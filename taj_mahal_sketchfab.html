Taj Mahal Heritage

UNESCO World Heritage Epitaph of Translucent Makrana Marble & Eternal Love

Pietra Dura Gemstone Inlays

Over 28 varieties of precious and semi-precious stones including lapis lazuli from Afghanistan, jade from China, turquoise from Tibet, and carnelian from Arabia were inlaid into polished Makrana marble.

Precision Mughal Symmetry

Every element of the mausoleum complex exhibits bilateral symmetry along a central axis, except for the cenotaph of Shah Jahan himself, which was positioned alongside Mumtaz Mahal decades later.

Earthquake-Safe Outward Minarets

The four 40-meter minarets were deliberately engineered to tilt slightly outward at a fraction of a degree, ensuring that in the event of a catastrophic earthquake, they would fall away from the central dome.
