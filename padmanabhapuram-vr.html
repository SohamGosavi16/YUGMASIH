Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple Heritage

Historic Sanctuary of Anantha Padmanabha & 100-Foot Dravidian Gopuram

Historic Royal Treasure Vaults

Six subterranean vaults (labeled A through F) house centuries of consecrated royal offerings, including a solid gold idol of Mahavishnu, Roman gold coins, and diamond-studded crowns.

12,008 Sacred Salagrama Stones

The main 18-foot reclining idol of Padmanabha is composed of 12,008 sacred Salagramas gathered from the Gandaki river in Nepal, covered in a traditional Ayurvedic plaster (Katusarkara yogam).

Ottakkal Mandapam Single Slab

The sanctum platform (Ottakkal Mandapam) was sculpted from a single massive granite slab measuring 2.5 feet thick and 20 feet square, transported from miles away and polished to mirror smoothness.
