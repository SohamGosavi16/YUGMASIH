Basilica of Bom Jesus Heritage

UNESCO World Heritage Baroque Architectural Monument of Goa

Silver Casket of St. Francis Xavier

Crafted by Goan silversmiths between 1636 and 1637, featuring 32 silver plaques depicting scenes from the saint's life, resting atop a three-tiered jasper and Italian marble mausoleum gifted by the Medici court of Florence.

Exposed Red Laterite Stone

Distinctive for its exposed, unplastered reddish-brown laterite facade with carved basalt orders and pediments, reflecting the fusion of European Baroque with coastal Indian craftsmanship.

54-Foot Gilded Altarpiece

The soaring golden high altar is dedicated to the Infant Jesus ('Bom Jesus') and St. Ignatius of Loyola, surrounded by intricate gold leaf woodwork carved from durable local rosewood.
