Stone Chariot in Hampi Heritage

UNESCO World Heritage Icon of the Vijayanagara Empire & Garuda Shrine

Revolving Granite Wheels

The four giant granite wheels are sculpted with delicate floral spokes and axle pins that once rotated freely on their stone axles before being cemented for structural preservation.

Emperor Krishnadevaraya's Triumph

Inspired by the Konark Sun Chariot during his military campaigns in Kalinga (Odisha), King Krishnadevaraya commissioned this shrine to recreate that cosmic glory in his capital on the Tungabhadra.

Musical Pillars of Vittala Temple

The adjacent Ranga Mandapa features 56 musical pillars (SaReGaMa pillars) carved from resonant granite clusters that emit notes of Indian musical instruments when tapped gently.
