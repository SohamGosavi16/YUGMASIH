Golden Temple (Sri Harmandir Sahib)

श्री हरिमन्दिर साहिब (स्वर्ण मंदिर)

Standing serenely in the centre of the sacred Amrit Sarovar ('Pool of Nectar'), Sri Harmandir Sahib was designed by Guru Arjan Dev Ji with four entrances welcoming people of all faiths. Rebuilt with gleaming gold-gilded copper plates and white marble inlays by Maharaja Ranjit Singh in 1830, embodying equality, peace, and eternal divine light.

State / Region Punjab (North India)
Historical Era 1581–1604 CE & 1830 CE
Commissioned / Dedicated To Guru Arjan Dev Ji & Maharaja Ranjit Singh
Geospatial Origin Amritsar, Punjab (Amrit Sarovar Pool)
