Indo-Saracenic Triumphal Basalt Arch

Gateway of India

गेटवे ऑफ़ इंडिया

Erected at Apollo Bunder overlooking the Arabian Sea, this 26-meter triumphal arch fuses 16th-century Gujarati architectural elements with classical Indo-Saracenic grandeur. Built from yellow basalt and reinforced concrete, it served as the ceremonial entrance for British viceroys and the ceremonial exit for the final British troops departing India in 1948.
