Brihadisvara Temple Heritage

UNESCO World Heritage Great Living Chola Temples & Monolithic Granite Vimana

80-Tonne Monolithic Granite Capstone

The apex dome (Kumbam) was sculpted from a single block of granite weighing approximately 80 tonnes, hauled up a 6-kilometer earthen ramp built through the town with elephants and rolling logs.

Natya Shastra Dance Sculptures

The first tier of the sanctum corridor contains 81 relief carvings of Lord Shiva performing the classical Karanas of Natya Shastra, preserving foundational postures of Bharatanatyam.

Exhaustive Royal Epigraphy

The granite basement plinth preserves thousands of lines of Tamil inscriptions detailing temple staff, musicians, 400 devadasi dancers, and exact endowments of gold and paddy down to tiny fractions.
