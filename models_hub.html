North India · Uttar Pradesh

Taj Mahal

Commissioned by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan for Empress Mumtaz Mahal. A symmetrical marvel of white Makrana marble, four outward-canted minarets, central onion dome, and intricate Pietra Dura semiprecious stone inlays.

Built For: Empress Mumtaz Mahal & Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan
Picked From: Agra, Uttar Pradesh (Yamuna Riverfront)
West India · Maharashtra

Ajanta & Ellora Caves

Ancient rock-cut Buddhist, Hindu, and Jain cave monuments excavated out of solid volcanic basalt cliffs. Renowned globally for timeless mural masterpieces, monolithic Kailasa temple engineering, and sacred Chaitya prayer halls.

Built For: Lord Buddha, Shiva & Jain Tirthankaras (Vakataka & Rashtrakuta Dynasties)
Picked From: Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra (Waghora Gorge)
West India · Gujarat

Rani Ki Vav

Commissioned by Queen Udayamati in memory of King Bhima I, this masterpiece of Maru-Gurjara stepwell architecture descends into seven subterranean terraces lined with over 500 principal sculptures celebrating Lord Vishnu.

Built For: King Bhima I (Built by Queen Udayamati) & Lord Vishnu
Picked From: Patan, Gujarat (Saraswati River Basin)
North India · Rajasthan

Amber Fort

Rising dramatically above Maota Lake amidst the rugged Aravalli hills, Amer Fort unites martial Rajput fortification with opulent courtly elegance, featuring marble courtyards, Sheesh Mahal mirror mosaics, and the Ganesh Pol gate.

Built For: Rajput Kachwaha Dynasty (Built by Raja Man Singh I)
Picked From: Amer, Jaipur, Rajasthan (Aravalli Hills)
East India · West Bengal

Victoria Memorial

Dominating the Kolkata Maidan, this grand white marble monument blends British, Mughal, Venetian, and Egyptian architectural influences. Crowned by a 16-foot rotating bronze Angel of Victory and surrounded by 64 acres of manicured gardens.

Built For: Queen Victoria, Empress of India (Commissioned by Lord Curzon)
Picked From: Kolkata, West Bengal (Kolkata Maidan)
South India · Tamil Nadu

Brihadisvara Temple

Consecrated by Emperor Raja Raja Chola I on the Cauvery delta, Peruvudaiyar Kovil stands as the supreme summit of Dravidian temple architecture, crowned by a 216-foot soaring granite tower sculpted from 130,000 tonnes of granite.

Built For: Lord Shiva (Consecrated by Emperor Raja Raja Chola I)
Picked From: Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu (Cauvery River Delta)
South India · Karnataka

Stone Chariot in Hampi

Standing majestically within the courtyard of the Vijaya Vittala Temple, this iconic shrine dedicated to Garuda is sculpted from interlocking granite slabs to resemble a colossal battle chariot, celebrated across the world as an emblem of Karnataka's golden age.

Built For: Garuda (Vahana of Lord Vishnu) & Vijayanagara Golden Age (King Krishnadevaraya)
Picked From: Hampi, Vijayanagara, Karnataka (Tungabhadra Basin)
East India · Odisha

Konark Sun Temple

Conceived as a colossal celestial chariot for the Sun God Surya, pulled by seven spirited horses on 24 intricately sculpted sundial wheels. Built of Khondalite and Chlorite stone, it embodies the peak of Kalinga temple architecture.

Built For: Surya (The Sun God) (Commissioned by King Narasimhadeva I)
Picked From: Konark, Puri, Odisha (Bay of Bengal Coast)
East India · Assam

Rang Ghar Amphitheater

Constructed by Swargadeo Pramatta Singha in the royal capital of Rongpur, Rang Ghar ('House of Entertainment') is a two-storied oval pavilion from which Ahom royalty enjoyed elephant fights, buffalo matches, and indigenous wrestling during the Rongali Bihu festivities.

Built For: Ahom Royal Dynasty (Built by Swargadeo Pramatta Singha)
Picked From: Sivasagar (Rongpur), Assam
South India · Kerala

Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple

Revered as one of the 108 sacred Divya Desams, blending Kerala and Dravidian temple architecture. Renowned for its 100-foot seven-tier Rajagopuram, 365 carved granite pillars in the corridor, and sacred underground royal treasure vaults.

Built For: Lord Padmanabhaswamy (Anantha Sayana Vishnu) (Travancore Royal Dynasty)
Picked From: Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala
East India · Bihar

Mahabodhi Temple

Marking the sacred spot where Prince Siddhartha Gautama attained supreme enlightenment under the Bodhi Tree. The 55-meter grand pyramidal temple stands as one of the oldest all-brick structures surviving in India.

Built For: Gautama Buddha (Site of Supreme Enlightenment; Emperor Ashoka)
Picked From: Bodh Gaya, Bihar (Niranjana River)
Central India · Chhattisgarh

Lakshmana Temple

Regarded as one of India's finest surviving red brick temples, built with precision-carved terracotta bricks on a massive stone jagati. Dedicated to Lord Vishnu, featuring intricate Krishnaleela doorframe reliefs and Pancharatha shikhara.

Built For: Lord Vishnu & King Harshagupta (Built by Queen Vasata)
Picked From: Sirpur, Mahasamund, Chhattisgarh (Mahanadi River Basin)
West India · Goa

Basilica of Bom Jesus

A masterpiece of Baroque and Mannerist architecture built of unplastered red laterite stone and Basalt. Houses the sacred relics of St. Francis Xavier in a silver casket inside a multi-tiered Florentine marble mausoleum.

Built For: St. Francis Xavier & The Infant Jesus (Jesuit Order of Goa)
Picked From: Old Goa (Velha Goa), Goa (Mandovi River)
South India · Andhra Pradesh

Lepakshi Veerabhadra Temple

Built in 1530 CE by royal treasurers Virupanna and Viranna under Vijayanagara Emperor Achyuta Deva Raya. Globally celebrated for its gravity-defying hanging pillar, colossal monolithic Basavanna Nandi, seven-hooded Nagalinga, and vibrant Puranic frescoes.

Built For: Lord Veerabhadra (Shiva) (Commissioned by Virupanna & Viranna)
Picked From: Lepakshi, Sri Sathya Sai District, Andhra Pradesh
West India · Gujarat

Statue of Unity

Soaring 182 meters (597 feet) above the sacred Narmada River facing the Sardar Sarovar Dam, this colossal bronze-clad colossus honors Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, the 'Iron Man of India' who unified 562 princely states into one sovereign nation. Sculpted by master artist Ram V. Sutar.

Built For: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel ('Iron Man of India') (Govt. of Gujarat / India)
Picked From: Sadhu Bet, Kevadia, Narmada District, Gujarat
North India · Uttarakhand

Jageshwar Dham Temple Cluster

Cluster of over 100 stone Latina Nagara temples nestled in a sacred Deodar cedar valley, housing the ancient Maha Mrityunjaya Mahadev shrine in the Kumaon Himalayas.

Built For: Lord Shiva (Maha Mrityunjaya) (Katyuri & Chand Dynasties)
Picked From: Almora, Uttarakhand (Jata Ganga Valley, Himalayas)
East India · Tripura

Neermahal Water Palace

Floating jewel of the Manikya kings built in the center of Rudrasagar Lake, fusing Hindu and Mughal pavilions with motorboat landing docks and royal water gardens.

Built For: King Bir Bikram Kishore Manikya Bahadur (Manikya Dynasty)
Picked From: Melaghar, Tripura (Rudrasagar Lake Island)
