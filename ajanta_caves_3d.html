Ajanta & Ellora Caves Heritage

UNESCO World Heritage Rock-Cut Monolithic Caves & Monasteries

Ancient Tempera Fresco Paintings

Ajanta's caves preserve India's most celebrated classical wall paintings, depicting Jataka tales with natural mineral pigments on clay and cow-dung plaster that have endured over two millennia.

Monolithic Kailasa Engineering

Neighboring Ellora Cave 16 (Kailasa) represents the largest monolithic rock excavation in human history, sculpted top-down from a single basalt cliff, removing 200,000 tonnes of volcanic rock without scaffolding.

Harmonious Religious Confluence

The caves exemplify India's historic pluralism, with 34 major excavations spanning Buddhist Chaityas, Hindu shrines, and Jain Indrasabha sanctuaries built in peaceful coexistence.
