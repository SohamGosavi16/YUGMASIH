Victoria Memorial Heritage

Makrana Marble Indo-Saracenic Monument & Cultural Epicenter

Makrana White Marble Quarry

Constructed with over 20,000 tonnes of pristine white marble transported across 1,500 km from the same Makrana quarries in Rajasthan that supplied the Taj Mahal three centuries earlier.

Rotating Bronze Angel of Victory

The central dome is topped by a 16-foot tall, three-tonne bronze figure of the Angel of Victory that rotates gracefully with the wind, acting as a functional weather vane.

Enormous Gallery of National Art

Houses 25 galleries containing rare oil paintings by Thomas and William Daniell, manuscript copies of Ain-i-Akbari, and extensive collections documenting the cultural history of the Bengal Renaissance.
