Lepakshi Veerabhadra Temple Heritage

Vijayanagara Architectural Wonder of Hanging Pillar & Monolithic Basavanna

Gravity-Defying Hanging Pillar

Among the 70 carved granite pillars in the Mukha Mandapa, one famous central pillar does not touch the ground. A thin cloth or paper can be slid completely underneath from one side to the other without obstruction.

Colossal Monolithic Basavanna (Nandi)

Located 200 meters from the temple complex, this 4.5-meter high and 8.2-meter long monolithic Nandi is carved from a single massive granite boulder, renowned as one of India's largest monolithic bull sculptures.

Vibrant Ceiling Murals of Vijayanagara

The ceilings of the Ardha Mandapa display breathtaking 16th-century fresco paintings using organic mineral pigments depicting 14 avatars of Lord Shiva, the Kiratarjuniya epic, and courtly ceremonies.
