Statue of Unity Heritage

World's Tallest Monument (182m) Dedicated to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

182-Meter Record-Breaking Scale

Rising 182 meters (597 feet) — precisely matching the 182 constituencies of the Gujarat Legislative Assembly — it stands almost four times taller than the Statue of Liberty (from base to torch) and twice the height of the Spring Temple Buddha.

Seismic & Wind-Resistant Structural Core

Engineered with twin reinforced concrete cores surrounded by a steel space frame, wrapped in 6,500 individual cast bronze panels (1,700 tonnes of bronze). Designed to withstand wind speeds of 180 km/h and Zone VI earthquakes.

Loha Campaign & Farmers' Contribution

The iron for the statue's base was contributed by over 100 million farmers from 169,000 villages across India who donated their used agricultural iron tools, symbolizing national unification.
