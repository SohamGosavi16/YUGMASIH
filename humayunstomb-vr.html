Humayun's Tomb Heritage

UNESCO World Heritage First Dynastic Mughal Garden Tomb

Sublime Charbagh Paradise Garden

Set within a 30-acre quadrilateral Persian garden divided into four main quadrants by causeways and waterways, symbolizing the lush Quranic gardens of Jannat.

Double Dome Architectural Feat

The first building on the Indian subcontinent to employ the double dome—a high exterior dome for grand scale and a proportionate lower interior ceiling for the burial chamber.

Precursor to the Taj Mahal

Introduced the octagonal chamber plan, monumental arched iwans, white marble inlays against red sandstone, and elevated podium later perfected at Agra in the Taj Mahal.

Monument Provenance & Heritage

This interactive 3D digital model showcases the living architectural heritage of Humayun's Tomb, located in Nizamuddin East, New Delhi (Yamuna Banks) and built for Mughal Empress Bega Begum for Emperor Humayun. Curated for interactive preservation and cultural learning.
