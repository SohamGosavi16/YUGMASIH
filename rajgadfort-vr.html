Rajgad Fort (राजगड किल्ला)

Perched dramatically at 1,376 meters in the Sahyadri range of Pune, Rajgad was the sovereign capital of the Maratha Empire under Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj for over 26 years. Known as the 'King of Forts' with formidable machis and the Balekilla citadel.

Rajgad Fort Heritage

First Capital of Maratha Empire & Sovereign Mountain Citadel

Balekilla Central Mountain Citadel

The crowning volcanic peak rising above clouds, fortified with rock-cut gates (Maha Darwaza), subterranean royal granaries, and the private residence of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

Suvela & Sanjeevani Machi Ramparts

Extending over kilometers of razor-sharp ridges, these machis feature double-curtained loopholed stone walls, hidden sally ports, and the legendary natural wind-tunnel rock opening (Nedhe).

Sovereign Capital of Swarajya

Chosen for its impregnable natural defenses, Rajgad witnessed the birth of the Maratha Navy, historic peace treaties, and was the royal seat during the foundational era of Maratha Swarajya.
