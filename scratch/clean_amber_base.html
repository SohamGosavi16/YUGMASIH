Amber Fort Heritage

UNESCO World Heritage Rajput Hill Fort & Mirror Palace Citadel

Sheesh Mahal (Hall of Mirrors)

The mirror palace is covered in thousands of concave imported convex mirrors that glitter brilliantly in candlelight, reflecting a single flame into a cosmic sea of stars.

Ganesh Pol Royal Frescoes

The majestic entrance gate is covered in vegetable-dye frescoes and latticed marble screens (Jalis) through which royal women could observe public durbars in privacy.

Subterranean Jaigarh Tunnels

An extensive network of subterranean escape tunnels links Amber Fort with Jaigarh Fort on the Cheel ka Teela peak above, safeguarding the Kachwaha royal family during sieges.
