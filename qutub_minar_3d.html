Qutub Minar Heritage

UNESCO World Heritage World's Tallest Brick Minaret (72.5m)

Five Fluted Tapering Storeys

The tower tapers from a 14.3m base to 2.7m at the peak. The lower three storeys feature alternating angular and circular flutings, while the top two tiers are cased in white marble.

Intricate Arabic Calligraphy

Continuous horizontal bands of decorative Kufic and Naskh Arabic inscriptions praise Sultan Mu'izz al-Din and record historical restorations by Firoz Shah Tughlaq and Sikandar Lodi.

The Rustless 4th-Century Iron Pillar

Standing in the adjacent courtyard, this 7.2-meter iron pillar forged during the Gupta Empire has resisted monsoon corrosion and oxidation for over 1,600 years without rust.
